# Portfolio content and imagery

The nine case studies use the project briefs supplied by Esgrow Studio on 21 October 2026. Delivery scope and qualitative business outcomes come from those briefs. No revenue, ROAS, conversion, traffic or adoption statistics have been inferred. Public counters and sample testimonials have not been reused as evidence.

Public websites were visually reviewed on the same date. Some Emergent previews expose only a frontend while their backend is asleep; reviewing them does not verify order processing, private dashboards or payment settlement. Private workflow descriptions use the studio's brief.

All `*-screen.webp` images are authentic browser captures, locally optimised to WebP. Other images are product/editorial imagery observed on the corresponding public website, including its existing third-party image sources. They are not newly generated products, testimonials or invented interface captures. Original product watermarks are retained where present. Sources: Esfire and Delta machine assets on their Cloudinary accounts; Swaad on its Emergent image host; Rasam on its linked Unsplash imagery; Shree Radhe on its public `/images` path. Images are served locally; portfolio visitors do not need to load the original project websites.

The Rasam seasonal control and ResQVerse role selector illustrate project workflows. They do not modify client data or connect to client admin accounts.
